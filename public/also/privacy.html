Privacy

Last updated October 24, 2026. This covers the unlisted Chrome extension.

Also shows a badge on a product photo when you, or a friend, has marked that product.

What is collected

  • Account. The email you sign in with, the name on your profile, and a friend code.
  • Marks you choose. When you mark a product as eyeing, obsessed, or caved, Also saves that product's URL, title, image, and price, and the reaction you picked.
  • Friends. People you add with a friend code, and the marks they share with you.
  • Settings on this browser. Whether new marks start private, whether creator notes are shown, and whether private view memory is on.

What is not collected

Pages you browse are not tracked. Opening a shop, scrolling a grid, or leaving a product unmarked does not send that page anywhere.

View memory is off unless you turn it on. If you do, a product you spend time on can be remembered for you. Friends never see those views.

Also does not read pages outside the shops it supports. It does not sell data. It does not share data for advertising.

Where it is stored

Your account, marks, and friendships are stored securely with Supabase, our database provider. A copy of your session and the product photos already shown stay in Chrome's local storage on your computer.

The creator notes that ship with Also are fictional examples. They are not your account, and they are not real endorsements.

Who can see a mark

A mark shared with friends is visible to your circle. A private mark is visible to you. New marks can start private.

Deletion

Sign out in settings to drop the session on this browser.

To delete the account and the marks stored in the project, email from the account address and ask for the Also account to be deleted. The account, its marks, and its friendships are removed. A friend's own marks stay theirs.
